About DocSense

We build the layer between raw documents and real decisions.

DocSense started with a familiar frustration: the answer is somewhere in a 300-page file, and nobody has an afternoon to find it. We set out to make document intelligence feel less like a search box and more like a careful colleague.

Today, teams in legal, finance, and research use DocSense to read faster, compare carefully, and show their work. Every answer stays attached to the page it came from.

Our operating principles

Clear evidence over confident guesses.

  • Verification first

    If an answer cannot point to its source, it does not ship. Citations are part of the product, not a feature.

  • Privacy by default

    Your documents are never used to train shared models. Access is scoped, logged, and removable.

  • Clarity over noise

    One useful answer beats ten plausible ones. We design for the decision, not the demo.
